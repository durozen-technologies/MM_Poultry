
# Broiler Wholesale Management App

## Customer Proposal

### Project Objective

This mobile application is designed for broiler wholesalers who purchase chickens from farms and supply them to multiple retail chicken shops every day. The system digitalizes the complete workflow from order booking to delivery, billing and weight-loss analysis.

## How the System Works

1. Every retailer receives a secure login.
2. Retailers place their daily chicken requirement in kilograms.
3. The wholesaler views all daily orders in one dashboard.
4. The wholesaler loads birds from the farm and records the total loading weight.
5. At each retailer location, a Bluetooth weighing scale automatically sends the actual delivery weight to the app.
6. A thermal printer instantly prints the delivery receipt.
7. Retailers can view delivered weight, bills, payment history and outstanding balance in their login.
8. The system automatically calculates total loaded weight, total delivered weight and weight loss.

## Key Features

- Retailer Login & Daily Orders
- Admin Dashboard
- Farm Load Entry
- Bluetooth Weight Scale Integration
- Delivery Management
- Thermal Receipt Printing
- Retailer Ledger & Outstanding Tracking
- Daily / Weekly / Monthly Reports
